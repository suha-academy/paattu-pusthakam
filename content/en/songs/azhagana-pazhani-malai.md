Azhagana Pazhani Malai Aandava -\
Unnai Anu Dinamum Paada Vandhaen Velava \
Valli Mayil Naadhane - Vaa Vadivelane\
Varavendum Mayil Meedhu Murugayyane\
Muruga...Muruga...Muruga...Muruga...

Velli Thiruneerum Vetri Vadivelum\
Ullathil Thondrume\
Velli Mayil Eri Velan Varum Vazhi\
Nenjam Kaanume\
Ennai Aalum Aandavane Ezhil Velava\
Ezhiyenum Unnai Paada Arulvai Ayya\
Muruga...Muruga...Muruga...Muruga...

(Azhagana Pazhani)

Nalladellam Ennai Naadi Perugida \
Nallarul Seivai\
Theeyadhellam Udan Theendhu Karugida Thiruvarul Purivai\
Unaiyandri Verillai Deivam Kandhayya\
Ulagaalum Aandavane Needhanayya\
Muruga...Muruga...Muruga...Muruga...

(Azhagana Pazhani)
